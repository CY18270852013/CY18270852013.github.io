利用夜间灯光遥感数据与人工智能方法评估台风经济损失及灾后恢复Assessing Typhoon Economic Losses and Post-Disaster Recovery with Nighttime Lights and AI 硕士学位研究 · 导师 张峰 教授M.S. research · Advisor: Prof. Feng Zhang

台风造成的经济损失通常滞后数周才被统计，且县级尺度记录零散。夜间灯光是经济活动的高频代理变量：台风过境后灯光亮度的下降幅度与恢复速度，可直接反映损失规模与恢复能力。本课题以多源夜光影像为观测，结合台风路径与强度、人口与经济、土地利用等数据，建立“损失—恢复”的评估框架，并检验其在未见台风事件上的泛化能力。Typhoon losses are usually reported weeks after landfall and are sparse at the county level. Nighttime lights provide a high-frequency proxy for economic activity: the magnitude of the brightness drop and the speed of recovery reflect loss and resilience. This project builds a loss–recovery framework from multi-source nighttime-light imagery combined with track, intensity, demographic, economic and land-use data.

  • 多源夜光影像预处理与一致性校正：NPP/VIIRS 月产品与珞伽一号影像，处理云污染、入射角与跨传感器定标。Preprocessing and harmonisation of NPP/VIIRS monthly composites and Luojia-1 imagery, including cloud masking, viewing-angle effects and cross-sensor calibration.
  • 以区县为单元的灯光异常提取，构建经济损失回归与分级模型，比较线性模型、树模型与深度时序网络的适用性。County-level light-anomaly extraction; regression and severity classification comparing linear, tree-based and deep sequence models.
  • 灾后恢复时序建模：估计恢复速度与恢复程度，分析强度、路径、经济水平与恢复差异的关系。Post-disaster recovery modelling: recovery speed and completeness as functions of intensity, track, economic level and region.
  • 与官方灾损统计及 EM-DAT 记录交叉验证，评估模型在不同台风与区域上的稳健性。Cross-validation against official loss statistics and EM-DAT records to test robustness across storms and regions.

热带气旋 “Freddy”（2023）生成过程中的热带波动作用分析Equatorial Wave Forcing during the Genesis of Cyclone Freddy (2023) 本科毕业论文 · 主持 · 指导 刘岩 助理教授B.Sc. thesis · Lead · Advisor: Dr. Yan Liu

Freddy 是南半球观测史上生命史最长的热带气旋之一（约 35 天），经历多次登陆与强度变化。论文按阶段分解其演变，研究 MJO 及 Kelvin、MRG、ER 等赤道波动对路径引导与强度维持的动力与热力贡献，解释其极端长生命史的主导机制。Cyclone Freddy is among the longest-lived tropical cyclones on record in the Southern Hemisphere (about 35 days). The thesis decomposes its evolution by stage and quantifies the dynamic and thermodynamic contributions of MJO, Kelvin, MRG and ER waves to track steering and intensity maintenance.

  • 波动滤波与多尺度分解：从再分析资料中分离 MJO / Kelvin / MRG / ER 信号。Wave filtering and multi-scale decomposition of MJO, Kelvin, MRG and ER signals from reanalysis data.
  • 涡度拉伸与垂直积分水汽输送（VIMD）诊断，量化波动对流与涡旋的相互作用。Vortex stretching and vertically integrated moisture divergence diagnostics to quantify wave–vortex interaction.
  • 对比两个阶段中波流相互作用差异，给出长生命史成因的阶段性解释。Stage-wise comparison of wave–flow interaction to explain the unusually long lifetime.

利用机器学习预测大气新粒子生成Machine Learning for Atmospheric New Particle Formation 核心成员 · 中芬科考项目 · 指导 齐西萌 助理教授Core member · China–Finland project · Advisor: Dr. Ximeng Qi

大气新粒子生成（NPF）事件的识别长期依赖人工判读。项目基于 Mask R-CNN 对 NPF 事件做自动检测与分类，自动计算生长率与起止时间，并统计不同类型事件在不同季节的分布。2025 年 8 月赴赫尔辛基参加科考与科研训练，向 Markku Kulmala 院士汇报成果。NPF event identification has long relied on manual inspection. This project applies Mask R-CNN to detect and classify NPF events, estimate growth rates and timing automatically, and analyse seasonal distributions. Results were presented to Prof. Markku Kulmala in Helsinki in August 2025.

  • 复现并改进基于 Mask R-CNN 的 NPF 事件检测流程，输出粒径谱可视化与掩膜结果。Reproduced and refined a Mask R-CNN pipeline for NPF detection with size-distribution visualisation and masks.
  • 自动计算 3–10 nm、10–25 nm、3–25 nm 三个粒径段的生长率，并判定事件起止时间。Automatic growth-rate estimation for 3–10 nm, 10–25 nm and 3–25 nm ranges, with event start and end times.
  • 分析不同类型 NPF 事件的季节分布规律。Seasonal distribution patterns across NPF event types.

中国风能太阳能资源评估及其未来变化趋势Wind and Solar Resource Assessment and Future Trends over China 主持人 · 省级大学生创新训练项目 · 指导 邱博 副教授Lead · Provincial undergraduate research project · Advisor: Dr. Bo Qiu

面向“双碳”目标下的新能源规划需求，融合地面观测、ERA5 再分析资料与 CMIP6 多模式输出，系统评估中国风速与地表短波辐射的时空变化特征，并预估风能、太阳能资源的空间分布与未来趋势。Combining station observations, ERA5 reanalysis and CMIP6 simulations to assess wind speed and surface shortwave radiation over China, and to project future wind and solar resources.

  • 构建中国区域风速与地表短波辐射的评估数据集，分析历史时空变化特征。Built an assessment dataset for wind speed and surface shortwave radiation over China.
  • 基于 CMIP6 多模式集合预估未来资源分布与趋势，并讨论预估的不确定性。Projected future resource distribution from the CMIP6 multi-model ensemble and discussed uncertainties.
