AI 与计算机CS & AI

这一页记录课程、教材、技术模块与代码实践。This page records courses, materials, technical modules and code practice.

代码实践Code Practice

把方法用在真实数据上是检验学习的方式。以下脚本均在研究中实际运行，涵盖读取、诊断、建模与绘图。Applying methods to real data is how I test what I learn. The scripts below were used in actual research, covering loading, diagnostics, modelling and plotting.

  • 降水与对流：GPM 半小时与逐日降水的合并、异常计算、Hovmöller 图与 OLR 分析。Precipitation and convection: merging GPM half-hourly and daily data, anomalies, Hovmöller diagrams and OLR analysis.
  • 赤道波动诊断：MJO、Kelvin、MRG、ER 滤波，涡度拉伸与垂直积分水汽输送。Equatorial waves: MJO, Kelvin, MRG and ER filtering, vortex stretching and moisture-divergence diagnostics.
  • 台风分析：路径与强度提取、生成区环境场、降水与 850 hPa 风场合成。Cyclone analysis: track and intensity extraction, genesis-region environments, rainfall and low-level wind composites.
  • 新能源资源：年平均风能资源、风速与比湿剖面、SST 及异常场处理。Renewable resources: annual wind energy, wind and humidity profiles, SST and anomaly processing.
  • 谱分析：调和分析、谱自由度估计与相干谱计算。Spectral analysis: harmonic analysis, spectral degrees of freedom and coherence.
