你好，我是陈勇Hello, I'm Yong Chen

复旦大学 · 大气与海洋科学系 · 硕士研究生M.S. student, Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences, Fudan University·导师 张峰 教授Advisor: Prof. Feng Zhang

我的研究关注台风：如何更准确地预报它的路径与强度，如何用卫星遥感与机器学习估计它造成的经济损失、刻画灾后恢复过程。方法上以深度学习与生成模型为主，也把智能体用于科研与工程工作流。I study typhoons: how to predict their tracks and intensity, and how to estimate their economic losses and recovery with satellite remote sensing and machine learning. My methods are mainly deep learning and generative models, with agents applied to research and engineering workflows.

本科毕业于南京大学大气科学学院（拔尖计划），现于复旦大学跟随张峰教授开展台风灾害的遥感—人工智能评估研究。I received my B.Sc. in Atmospheric Science from Nanjing University and now work with Prof. Feng Zhang at Fudan University.
